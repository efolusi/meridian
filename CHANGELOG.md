# Changelog

## Unreleased

Semver level: MINOR.

Meridian 2.6.0 in progress: the shared pieces of the design sync audit of 2026-11-28. No version is bumped yet; the release prep is its own change. Batch A is four small, library-only changes. Batch B adds the language switch and the footer legal row.

- **`--accent-word` is one token for the accent coloured word (MD109).** The accent word of a heading or a script title (the copper word in "Tagihan yang *jelas*") had no Meridian token. sso web, efolusi.com and the toolips site each remapped their own `--accent-word`, and two of them pointed it at `--brand-400` (`#CE9C66`) in dark, a valid ramp stop but a few hue steps off `--accent-edge` (`#CD8F69`). `tokens/base.css` now sets `--accent-word: var(--accent-edge)` on `:root` and on every `[data-theme]` element, so it resolves the `--accent-edge` of the theme the element sits in: `#A0693C` in light, `#CD8F69` in dark, and the `prefers-contrast: more` values (`#443D32`, `#EFCFAC`). In light it is the colour products already drew. A Meridian-level token like `--gap-label-icon`, not a vendored one. It is in [guidelines/theming.md](guidelines/theming.md#the-accent-word) and pinned by `tests/md109-accent-word.test.mjs`.
- **One page-title rule (MD113).** Pay and efolusi.com wrote Title Case and the products used three separators (pipe, colon, middle dot). The voice guideline (`guidelines/brand.md`, "Content fundamentals") now says: sentence case, the page first and the product name last, separated by ` · ` (a middle dot with a space on each side), never a pipe, a colon or a dash; the same in English and Indonesian ("Invoices · Meridian", "Tagihan · Meridian"). `tests/md113-page-title-rule.test.mjs` pins the words of the rule. Words only: no component changes.
- **`Badge` has typed `success` and `brand` variants (MD114).** sso web wrote the shipped classes `ef-badge--success` and `ef-badge--brand` by hand on a default Badge. `BadgeVariant` now lists `'success'` and `'brand'` (`Badge.d.ts`), `Badge.prompt.md` and the docs demos use `variant="success"` and `variant="brand"`, and the library's own demos, cards and showcases that wrote the class by hand use the variant. The rules and the look do not change (the classes were already there; a default Badge with the class and the variant alone draw the same). `scripts/check_contrast.py` now gates the pairs the two variants paint in all four modes (light, dark and both `prefers-contrast: more` tables): `success` text on its tint is 4.73, 6.06, 5.05 and 6.06:1; `brand` is cocoa on cream, 11.98:1 in light and in the increased light table, and in dark and the increased dark table the accent tint pair, 9.82:1. The existing variants do not change.
- **The `Checkbox` row is 24px at a fine pointer (MD115, WCAG 2.5.8).** At a fine pointer the row was 19.59px tall (the label line, 14px at 1.4; a box with no label 19px) and passed only by the spacing exception. At a fine pointer the row, the label and the hit area of the box are now 24px at least: `.ef-check` gets `min-height: 24px`, the label gets `min-height: 24px` and the block padding that fills the rest of its 1.4em line, the box keeps its 18 x 18 size and sits 2px lower to stay centred in the row (`margin-top: 3px`, it was 1px), and its `::before` hit area is 24 x 24. Measured in a browser at 1280px with a fine pointer: a labelled row 19.59 to 24px, a row with a description 37.79 to 42.18px, a bare box 19 to 24px; the box is 18 x 18 before and after. At a coarse pointer (390px) nothing changes: the 44 x 44 hit area and the row heights are as before. CSS only, in `Checkbox`; `tests/md115-checkbox-row-height.test.mjs` pins the rule and `tests/fixtures/md115-checkbox-row.html` measures it by hand.
- **`LanguageSwitch` in the marketing group (MD112).** pay, efolusi.com and the komando site each drew the ID and EN switch by hand: a small plain-text link, 13.7 to 17.2px wide and 15px high on pay, 40px on efolusi.com, the full language name twice on the komando site, and none with `hreflang`. `LanguageSwitch` renders a `nav` named "Language" ("Bahasa" with `lang="id"`) with a list of one item per language: `languages` is an array of `{ code, label, href, name? }`, `current` the code of the page's language, `size` is `md` (default) or `sm`. Each other language is an `<a href hreflang lang>` showing its short code (`ID`, `EN`), with the language spelled in itself as its accessible name and the visible code inside it ("Bahasa Indonesia (ID)", "English (EN)"; WCAG 2.5.3). `name` is built in for `en` and `id`, falls back to `Intl.DisplayNames` for another code, then to the label. The current language is not a link: `<span role="link" aria-disabled="true" aria-current="true" lang>`, as `BreadcrumbPage` is, so no link points at the page you are on. Targets: `md` is the 44px `--tap-min` circle of the `ThemeToggle` at every pointer; `sm` is `--control-h-sm`, 28px at a fine pointer, 24px in compact density and 44px at a coarse pointer. Measured in a browser: `md` 44 x 44 at fine and coarse pointers; `sm` 28.7 x 28 and 33.7 x 28 at fine (24px high in compact), 44 x 44 at coarse (375px). It renders plain anchors, as `SiteHeader` and `SiteFooter` do: the repo has no `linkComponent` convention (`asChild` belongs to compound parts such as `BreadcrumbLink`), and a language change loads the page in the other language. The focus ring is the library's, the only motion is the 120ms colour transition (off under reduced motion), and the component uses no `id`, so `SiteHeader` can render it twice in `actions`. `tests/language-switch.test.jsx` pins the markup, `tests/language-switch-targets.test.mjs` the 44 and 24px rule, `tests/language-switch-contract.test.mjs` the export, and `tests/fixtures/md112-language-switch.html` measures it by hand.
- **`SiteFooter` has a legal row and a computed copyright year (MD111).** The komando site, the efolusi.com legal pages and pay (partly) had no legal row in the footer, and a typed year: `© 2026` in a dictionary or a build step that someone edits every January. `SiteFooter` takes `name`, `year`, `since`, `legal` and `languageSwitch`. Every existing prop is unchanged, and a footer that passes only `bottom` renders the same markup as before. A legal link is `--control-h-sm` square at least: measured in a browser 28px high at a fine pointer (24px in compact density), 44 x 44 at a coarse pointer (375px). `tests/site-footer-legal.test.jsx` pins the row with a pinned year and with a mocked clock, `tests/site-footer-legal-targets.test.mjs` the target size, and `tests/site-footer-no-typed-year.test.mjs` fails on any four-digit year in the component, its types, its prompt or its docs example. `tests/fixtures/md111-site-footer-legal.html` measures it by hand.

## v2.5.2 - 2026-11-28

Semver level: PATCH.

- **A classless link inside navigation is plain again (MD97, defect of v2.5.1).** The MD92 rule underlined every classless anchor in an `li`, so it also reached the `li` of a `nav`: a header or footer nav list, a breadcrumb `ol` and a menu whose links carry no class (the product site had it on 112 pages), although MD92 says navigation lists are not covered. `tokens/base.css` now skips a link that has any of `nav`, `[role=navigation]`, `[role=menu]`, `[role=menubar]`, `[role=tablist]` or `[role=toolbar]` as an ancestor, at any depth, with `:not(:is(...) *)` inside the same `:where()`, so the specificity stays 0,0,1 and the hover rule has the same exclusion. `menubar` and `toolbar` are in the list because a link there is a control, like `menu` and `tablist`. The syntax needs Chrome 88, Firefox 84 or Safari 9, inside the supported browsers. A link in a `p`, a plain `li` or a `td` outside those ancestors is underlined as before, and a link with a class keeps its opt-out. **What a consumer does:** delete your own nav link-underline overrides (the product site's W55 rule, `nav li a { text-decoration: none }` and the like). Checked in a browser on a fixture of 14 cases (`tests/fixtures/md97-nav-links.html`, also read by `scripts/smoke_headless.mjs`): nine navigation cases went from underlined to none, and `p`, plain `li` and `td` stay underlined. Visual baselines move where a screenshot shows a classless link inside a navigation list; they are regenerated by the update-visual-baselines workflow in CI, not by hand.
- design-tokens stays vendored at v0.8.0; this release changes no token.

## v2.5.1 - 2026-11-28

Semver level: PATCH.

- **Links in `li`, `td`, `caption`, `label` and an `Alert` description are underlined (MD92, WCAG 1.4.1).** The v2.5.0 rule underlined classless anchors in `p`, `dd`, `blockquote` and `figcaption` only, so a link inside a list item, a table cell, a table caption, a consent label (`<label>I accept the <a>terms</a></label>`) or the text of an `Alert` (the product site had 18 in `li` and `td`; sso `/account` had Platform terms and Privacy notice in an `Alert`) was colour only, and the link colour is 2.43:1 against body ink in light and 1.88:1 in dark. `tokens/base.css` now covers those parents with the same thin underline (thicker on hover) and the same `:not([class])` opt-out, so a navigation list whose links carry a class stays plain. Deliberately not covered: `th` (a header is a label, its link is a sort or navigation control), `summary` (the disclosure control), `dt` and the `ul`, `ol` and `nav` containers. **What a consumer does:** delete your own scoped rule that underlines a classless link in `li` or `td` (the product site has one, row W52), and in an `Alert` description. A link that must stay plain inside one of these parents takes a class.
- **The password reveal button of `Input` is concentric with the field (MD93).** Its radius was a fixed `--radius-sm` (10px), whatever the field drew, so under `data-shape="pill"` (field 16px) it was the wrong curve for its corner. It is now `calc(var(--field-radius, var(--radius-sm)) - var(--space-1))`: the field radius minus the 4px the button sits in from the field edge, so it follows `data-shape`. It still does not read `--press-radius` (a part inside a field is not a button on its own). Measured in a browser: 6px in the default shape (field 10px) and 12px under `data-shape="pill"` (field 16px), at 1280 and at 375; it was 10px in both. The size does not change: 28px square at 1280, 44px on a coarse pointer (375), where it is as high as the field and the radius follows the inline inset only. A default-shape field's reveal button is 4px less round. **What a consumer does:** delete your own `.ef-input__reveal { border-radius }` override if you wrote one to match the pill shape; Playwright baselines of a revealable `Input` move.
- **Visual baselines move.** Every Playwright screenshot that shows a classless link in a list item, a table cell, a caption, a label or an `Alert` description, and every revealable password `Input`, changes (an underline, and a 6px or 12px reveal corner). They are regenerated by the update-visual-baselines workflow in CI, not by hand.
- **Bundle size (MD92, MD93).** Against main at 1bf2af39: `_ds_bundle.js` +43 bytes (918,512 to 918,555), `dist/components.css` +43 bytes (250,054 to 250,097), the npm total +684 bytes (1,586,812 to 1,587,496), the CLI package unchanged (49,924). `scripts/size_budget.json` is re-recorded to these sizes.

## v2.5.0 - 2026-11-27

Semver level: MINOR.

The interface accent is now the owl's copper, `#CD8F69`, in both themes, and the `ember` ramp is gone (design-tokens v0.8.0, vendored in this release). Primary buttons, links, the focus ring and chart lines change colour for every consumer. Six token names are removed with no alias, which breaks any stylesheet that names them; while Meridian is used only inside Efolusi that counts as a minor, and the visual language (glass, paper, radius, type, layout) stays. One list, for a consumer:

- **Breaking: six token names are removed (MD74).** `--ember-50`, `--ember-100`, `--ember-150`, `--ember-300`, `--ember-600` and `--ember-700` (in `tokens.json`: `ember.50` and so on). A `var()` that points at a missing property is silently invalid and falls back to the inherited colour, so search your own CSS for `ember`. The migration section is [guidelines/migration.md](guidelines/migration.md#v250).
- **Five new names.** `--copper-300` `#E7B496`, `--copper-400` `#DBA582`, `--copper-500` `#CD8F69`, `--copper-600` `#BD805A`, and `--accent-edge`: `#A0693C` in light, the fill itself in dark, `#443D32` and `#EFCFAC` with `prefers-contrast: more`. Fifteen existing tokens change value (`--accent` and its hover, active, tint, tint border, contrast and tint text, `--text-link` and its hover, `--focus-ring-color`, `--surface-brand` and its hover, `--field-1`, `--field-2`, `--text-on-brand`); the exact values are in design-tokens v0.8.0. The vendored files also carry `--text-3xs`, `--text-4xs`, `--control-h-sm-plus` and `--control-h-md-plus` (DT01, DT02), which no component reads yet.
- **What you see.** Primary buttons, the solid `IconButton`, `Toolbar` active, `TabBar` current, the `Calendar` selected day, send buttons and the accept buttons of `ToolCall` and `Confirmation` are copper with an ink label (6.37:1; the label was white on ember). Links are brown (`#7A4E2B`) in light and peach-copper in dark. The focus ring is brown in light and copper in dark, the same 2px plus 2px shape. Tints (`Badge` accent, selected rows, `Calendar`, `Toggle`) are cream-tan instead of peach. `LineChart` and `BarChart` default to copper. The warm band and the page glows are cream instead of peach. Playwright baselines move.
- **Shapes paint `--accent-edge` (MD74).** A copper fill is 2.47 to 2.72:1 on the light surfaces, under the 3:1 a shape needs (WCAG 1.4.11). Every shape a component draws with the accent now paints `--accent-edge` (4.17 to 4.58:1 on page, card, sunken and subtle, 3.58 on a track, 3.79 on the border ground; in dark it is the fill, so nothing moves): the 1px edge of a filled `Button` primary, `IconButton` solid, `Toolbar`, `TabBar`, `Calendar` day, `Badge` solid, `Questionnaire` primary, send buttons, `Player` play and the file-input button; the check marks and fills of `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `Steps`, `Todo`, `AgentRun` and `ButtonTile`; the `Radio` dot and border, the `Slider` thumb and range; the selected and focused borders of the inputs, `ButtonTile`, `Questionnaire`, `FileDrop` and `Item`; thin indicators (`Progress`, `UsageMeter`, `Tabs`, `PageControl`, `Steps` line, `Player` bars, `Resizable` handle, chart lines). `Bubble` and the `ChatMessage` avatar stay plain copper: words sit beside them and no control draws on them. The `Slider` rail and the unplayed `Player` bars use `--surface-track`, so the range keeps 3:1 on them (`--border-strong` gave 2.95:1). The rail and the unplayed bars are weaker against the card for it, 1.28:1 in light, accepted: the thumb edge (4.58:1) and the range edge (3.58:1) carry the value. They were 1.55:1 and 1.49:1 on `--border-strong` and the old sand. `scripts/check_contrast.py` gates `--accent-edge` in light, dark and both `prefers-contrast: more` tables on every ground a group of components sits on (323 to 397 pairs); `tests/accent-edge-shapes.test.mjs` fails a component that paints the bare accent as a border, outline, stroke, mark or fill with no edge. `--accent-warm` (2.89:1 on the page) and `--accent-soft` (1.83:1) are decoration only: no component reads them, and a test keeps them out of every border, fill, stroke and text colour.
- **Forced-colors mode keeps the edge (MD74).** The edge of an accent fill is an inset `box-shadow` in `Switch`, `Toolbar`, `Player` and the file-input button, and forced-colors mode drops box-shadow, so a checked `Switch` had no fill and no edge. In forced-colors mode the four now draw `outline: 1px solid` in a system colour (`Highlight` for the selected `Switch` and `Toolbar` button, `ButtonText` for the `Player` play button and the file-input button). `Switch`, `Toolbar` buttons and `Player` buttons also get a 2px `Highlight` focus outline there, from the one base rule (see MD78): they set `outline: none` on `:focus-visible` and draw the focus ring as a shadow, so a focused checked `Switch` or active `Toolbar` button had no focus indicator at all.
- **`BarChart` base bars read `--border-control`, and the highlight carries an outline (MD74).** The base bars use the form-control edge (3.30:1 on the page and 3.42:1 on the card in light, 3.72:1 and 3.39:1 in dark, so a base bar still holds 3:1) instead of `--text-muted` (5.83:1 on the page in light, 6.67:1 in dark). The highlighted bars (`highlightLast`) are copper (`--accent`) with a 1px `--accent-edge` outline, like every other filled accent shape, and the base bars have none (a `forced-colors` outline keeps it there). Tone cannot carry the highlight alone (WCAG 1.4.1): against the base it is 1.34:1 in light (it was 1.32) and 1.75:1 in dark (it was 1.03), and 3:1 is out of reach with one colour, because the base needs 3:1 from the page, the bronze edge is itself 4.43:1 (light) and 6.49:1 (dark) from it, and the only colours 3:1 from both are near-black in light and none in dark. So the outline carries it, `check_contrast.py` prints the tone pair as a labelled mark (the bars also keep their hidden data table) and holds it at 3:1 in both `prefers-contrast: more` tables (3.14:1 and 3.21:1). `BarChart` draws no value label on a bar (its labels are axis ticks, spread edge to edge), so there is no bar label to strengthen. The highlighted `BarChart` bar is told apart by hue plus a 1px edge, about 1.3:1 by tone in light, weak for colour-blind readers: pair it with a label or a legend.
- **Links in running text are underlined (MD74, WCAG 1.4.1).** The link colour is 2.43:1 against body ink in light and 1.88:1 in dark, so colour alone does not mark a link. `Link` is underlined (the underline thickens on hover instead of going away; `standalone`, the lone call to action with an arrow, has none), and classless anchors inside `p`, `dd`, `blockquote` and `figcaption` are underlined by `tokens/base.css`. The `quiet` variant is for navigation and footers, never running text: it is plain at rest and underlined on hover and keyboard focus, so a `Link` in a footer or a nav list stays plain. `LegalDocument` already underlined.
- **A disabled primary `Button` and solid `IconButton` are flat neutral (MD74).** They had no state of their own: the shared `opacity: .45` left the ink label 1.91:1 from the copper fill in light and 2.31:1 in dark. They now paint `--surface-track` with a `--text-muted` label, 4.71:1 in light and 4.76:1 in dark, gated in `check_contrast.py` (WCAG exempts disabled controls; the label stays legible anyway). A loading primary `Button` keeps full strength (opacity 1, ink on copper 6.37:1, a progress cursor, hover and press leave it alone): its spinner is the only busy cue and faded copper was 1.9:1. `IconButton` treats `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-disabled` like `disabled`, as `Button` does.
- **`CardTitle` is a heading (MD70, WCAG 1.3.1).** It was a `div`, so a page of cards had no headings. It renders an `h2` and takes `as` (`h1` to `h6`) for the level the page needs; the look is the same. This adds headings for every consumer, which can change a page's heading outline: pass `as` where `h2` does not fit. The `CardTitle` ref and attribute types moved from `HTMLDivElement` to `HTMLHeadingElement` in `Card.d.ts`.
- **`Alert` draws a default icon and has no empty gap (MD71, WCAG 1.4.1).** Each variant shows a glyph unless you pass your own `Icon`, `svg` or `data-slot` icon: `default` info, `success` circle-check, `warning` triangle-alert, `danger` circle-alert, so state is not colour alone. New props: `icon="name"` for another glyph, `icon={false}` for none. An alert with no icon is one column with no column gap (it kept a 12px gap and an empty column, so the text sat 12px in for nothing). `variant` now lists `success` and `warning` in the types; the classes already existed. A default `Alert` that had no icon now shows the info icon: pass `icon={false}` to keep it plain. `Alert` finds its parts by class and by descendant, not as direct children, so a wrapper element around the icon, title, description or action (the starters' template runtime wraps every part in a `display:contents` element) no longer breaks the grid: before, the description sat in a 0px column there. The default icon steps aside for any `data-slot="icon"` in the alert that is not inside its title, description or action. A third-party or wrapped icon without `data-slot="icon"` is not recognised as yours, so the alert shows its own as well: carry `data-slot="icon"` on your icon, or pass `icon={false}`.
- **`Table` fades its end edge while content is hidden (MD73).** At 375px a table with many columns scrolled with no cue. The scroll wrapper now fades its inline-end edge (28px) while more content lies past it, shrinking to nothing over the last 40px of the scroll. CSS only: a scroll-driven animation resizes a mask, with no scroll listener and no layout read; a table that fits is not masked, a browser without scroll-driven animations shows no fade, right-to-left mirrors, and the fade lifts while the wrapper, a focused row or a control in the last column has keyboard focus, so no focus ring sits under it. `prefers-reduced-motion` leaves the fade on on purpose: a scroll timeline ignores the near-zero animation duration, and nothing moves in time. The role, tab stop and `label` prop are unchanged.
- **`Badge` colours follow the tokens (MD49).** The dark `accent` and `brand` badge text read `--brand-300`; they read `--accent-subtle-fg` now (`#EFCFAC` in dark, 0.004 from the owl's tan). The raw ramp allowlist (`scripts/ramp_allowlist.json`) falls from 66 uses in 24 files to 61 in 23: four in `Badge`, one in `Player`.
- **What a consumer does.** Bump `@efolusi/meridian` to exactly this version and rebuild. Search your own CSS for `--ember-` and replace each use (a fill is `--accent`, text is `--text-link`, the new ramp is `--copper-300` to `--copper-600`). Where your code draws the accent as a border, icon, line or thin fill on a light surface, use `--accent-edge`. Look at primary buttons, checked states, tabs, links and charts in light and dark; regenerate screenshot tests that show an accent fill, a link or a default `Alert`. Check heading levels where you use `CardTitle`. Re-run your own contrast checks if you remap the accent group: `--accent-edge` is a seventh token to set. [guidelines/theming.md](guidelines/theming.md) lists the pairs.
- **`Badge` `default` is neutral (MD69).** It was `background: var(--accent)` with `var(--accent-contrast)` text, the same fill as the primary button, so a list of normal statuses (Verified, Admin) was louder than the one exception (Suspended) and sat in the same colour as the Save button beside it. It is now a sunken surface (`--surface-sunken`) with a strong border (`--border-strong`) and ink text (`--text-primary`); the border darkens to `--border-hover` on hover. Text contrast is 15.75:1 in light and 12.65:1 in dark. `secondary` (no border, secondary text), `outline` (border, no fill) and the other variants are unchanged and stay distinct. The colour classes (`ef-badge--accent`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, `--brand`) now also reset the border, so they look the same on a `default` badge as before. Use `variant="solid"` for the old look.
- **To keep the old look, use `variant="solid"` (MD69).** New variant `solid` is the filled accent badge the old `default` was, in copper (`--accent` fill, `--accent-contrast` text, `--accent-hover` on hover, and the 1px `--accent-edge` border since MD74): 6.37:1 in both themes at rest, 8.01:1 on hover. ## v1.5.2 - 2026-07-20

### Fixed
- **Server rendering any component with a proportional inline style mismatched on hydration.** Percentages were built as `value + '%'`, which writes every digit JavaScript has: `1/3 * 100` becomes `33.33333333333333%`. Browsers keep about six significant figures when parsing an inline style, so the DOM reads back `33.3333%`, React compares its own string against the browser's truncated one, and reports a mismatch. React does not patch these up, so the affected elements keep the server's values and any client-side recalculation is silently dropped.

  `Player` hit it on every one of its 72 waveform bars, `UsageMeter` on any ratio that does not divide evenly, `BarChart` on essentially all real data, `Resizable` on a non-half split, and `LineChart` on its hover tooltip. Reported from a downstream Next.js App Router build.

  Percentages now go through a shared internal `cssPct` helper that rounds to three decimals, comfortably inside what every parser preserves and far below one pixel at any realistic size. Rounding alone is not sufficient: a fixed-decimal format would emit `33.300%`, which browsers normalise to `33.3%` and which mismatches for the same reason, so the helper relies on number-to-string dropping trailing zeros.

  Regression coverage renders the five components with `renderToString` and asserts no serialised style carries more decimals than the parser keeps. Note for future work: jsdom preserves full float precision where browsers truncate, so a round-trip assertion through jsdom passes even with the fix reverted. The test asserts the precision bound directly for that reason.

- **Every icon in the npm package was blank.** 68 of the 106 components render an `Icon`, and the tarball shipped none of the 108 SVGs. Even had it shipped them, `Icon` derives its base URL from the `<script src="..._ds_bundle.js">` tag, which exists only on the zero-build site: in an npm consumer that lookup returns `''` and the request goes out page-relative. Copying the files in would not have fixed the URL, so the npm build now inlines the SVG sources (~38 kB raw, ~5 kB gzipped) and drops the fetch entirely. Icons render in server output instead of one effect late, with no request per icon per route.
- **`styles.css` used bare `@import "tokens/…"` specifiers.** A browser reads those as relative URLs, which is why the CDN path never noticed, but a bundler CSS pipeline can read a non-relative specifier as a module request into `node_modules`. Reported from a real downstream build. Now `./tokens/…`, which is correct everywhere.
- **No `"use client"` directive**, so importing the package from a Next.js App Router Server Component was a build error. Every component calls hooks; the directive is now emitted on each module and is inert outside RSC.
- **`LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` did not ship.** A `"license": "MIT"` field is metadata, not the grant; MIT requires the text to travel with the code. The Lucide licence ships with the inlined icons for the same reason.
- `sideEffects` was `*.css`, a glob that does not cross a directory boundary and so missed `tokens/*.css`. Now `**/*.css`.
- `assets/*` was unreachable through `exports`: the `./*` pattern rewrote every non-JS subpath to a `.js` that does not exist.
- **Deep imports were broken in the published 1.5.0.** The `exports` map used a single `./*` pattern, which appends the extension, so `@efolusi/meridian/forms/Button.js` resolved to `forms/Button.js.js` and threw for every consumer — and that is the exact spelling the package README documents. `@efolusi/meridian/package.json` failed the same way, which breaks tools that read it. Both spellings now resolve, with or without `.js`.
- `check_npm_package.mjs` imported `dist/` by file path, which bypasses `exports` entirely; that is why the broken map shipped green. It now symlinks the build into a temp `node_modules` and resolves every specifier by package name, the way Node does after an install.

## v1.5.0 - 2026-07-20

The runtime layer the 1.4.0 release was missing: a toast owner, field wiring, a portal for every floating surface, a real test suite, and an npm package whose types and assets actually work for consumers.

### Added
- **Toaster + `Toaster.useToast()`** — one owner for the toast queue: ids, timers, the live region and the portal. A toast carrying an action never auto-dismisses (WCAG 2.2.1), timers pause on hover and focus, and the stack holds a single live region instead of one per toast.
- **FormField + the field wiring** — label / hint / error / required chrome and the id that links them. All eleven form controls inherit `id`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid` and `aria-required` from an enclosing field; `group` mode uses `role="group"` for control sets; a render prop wires non-Meridian controls.
- **Table** gains sorting (applied by the table, with `sortAccessor`), selection with an indeterminate select-all, sticky header, and empty/loading states.
- **Portal + `useAnchoredStyle`**, and all eight floating surfaces routed through them: they flip when there is no room, shift to stay on screen, and can no longer be clipped by a scrolling ancestor.
- **A test suite** — 22 vitest + testing-library tests over the keyboard and wiring contracts that were previously only claimed in prose. Run with `npm test`.
- **An ESM npm build** (`npm run build:npm` → `@efolusi/meridian` in `dist/`), verified in CI by importing it. Not published to the registry yet.
- **The compiler**, vendored in as `scripts/build_bundle.mjs`; it reproduces the historical bundle byte for byte, and CI now rebuilds and diffs rather than trusting a recorded hash.
- Browser-support and RTL statements in `guidelines/accessibility.md` — including the honest note that RTL is *not* supported in 1.x.
- **A types gate** (`npm run check:types`, `scripts/check_types.mjs`). The `.d.ts` layer is hand-maintained, so nothing forced it to stay valid; it now compiles in CI, along with the emitted package's type barrel imported the way a consumer would.

### Changed
- Every component forwards unknown props to its root (104 of 106), and the nine you most often need a handle on forward refs.
- The six hand-rolled toast queues in the showcases are gone; tones are chosen per call instead of everything being green.

### Fixed
- The manifest's `components` and `tokens` inventories are generated rather than hand-maintained. They had drifted, which silently produced no registry item for Portal and dropped it from the dependency list of all eight overlays that import it.
- `Portal` imported `react-dom` explicitly instead of relying on a window global, which only worked inside the bundle.
- **The published types now compile for consumers.** `JSX.Element` was written against the global `JSX` namespace that `@types/react` 19 removed, so every declaration broke for anyone on React 19 — which `peerDependencies: react >=18` invites. All 102 uses are now `React.JSX.Element`, valid on both majors. Six interfaces also contradicted the DOM attributes they extended (`Card.title` as a node vs `string`; `size` as a scale vs `number` on Input, InputGroup, Select and Switch; `InputGroup.prefix` as a node), and the package's type barrel re-exported `.d.ts` paths, which TypeScript rejects outright (TS2846).
- **The npm package shipped CSS that pointed at fonts it did not contain.** `tokens/fonts.css` `@font-face`s five families from `../assets/fonts/`, but the build only copied components and token CSS. An unresolvable `url()` is a hard build failure in Vite and webpack, so the package would have broken every bundler consumer on first import. The fonts now ship (with their OFL texts, as the licence requires), and `check_npm_package.mjs` fails on any CSS asset missing from the tarball.
- **Repo-wide factual drift.** An audit of every surface against measured counts fixed 83 confirmed items: component inventories that listed 104 of 106 (README omitted `FormField` and `Portal` from their groups), a stale icon count of 65 against the 108 that ship, `unexposedExports` documented as three helpers when the bundle carries seven, `kits`/`templates` named in the license scope years after they became `showcases`/`starters`, ROADMAP entries still listing shipped work as missing, docs-site meta and JSON-LD counts, `.prompt.md` examples referencing an icon that does not exist, and the last of the internal-product naming. Released changelog entries were deliberately left alone: they record what was true at the time.

## v1.4.0 - 2026-07-19 - first public release

The first tagged, installable version. Meridian is repositioned as a general-purpose design system, the accessibility claims it was making are now true, and the documented install path actually runs.

### Fixed
- **Focus indicator now meets WCAG 1.4.11.** The ring was translucent and measured 2.02–2.11:1 (light) and 2.61–2.77:1 (dark) against every surface, failing the 3:1 non-text minimum on every focusable element.
